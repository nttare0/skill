Kigali started car-free days in 2016. On the first Sunday of every month, parts of the main roads close to traffic, and people walk, run, or cycle in the streets instead.

The idea came out of the government's push to cut air pollution and get people moving. It costs the city little to run: a few barriers, some police, and a handful of street sweepers after the crowds leave. Vendors set up stalls along the route, and aerobics classes take over the tarmac.

Attendance is high enough that the city has kept the event going every month since. There is no official count of participants, but the streets are full from early morning until evening. The plan is to extend the closed roads to more neighborhoods in the coming years, though nothing has been announced yet.
